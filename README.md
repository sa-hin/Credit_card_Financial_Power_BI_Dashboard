## Credit Card Transaction and Customer Report:

## ➡️ Overview:
This project involves the creation of comprehensive Power BI reports to visualize and analyze credit card customer and transaction data. 
The reports provide insights into various metrics, such as total revenue, interest earned, customer demographics, and transaction volumes. These reports were created using Power BI Desktop.

## ➡️ Features:

## 📌 Credit Card Transaction Report:
- Total Revenue, Total Interest, Transaction Amount, and Transaction Count.

- Quarterly Revenue and Transaction Count, Total Interest, Trasaction Amount.

- Revenue by Customer Job and Expenditure Type.

- Revenue by Education Level and Use Chip.

- Customer Acquisition Cost by Card Category.

## 📌 Credit Card Customer Report:

- Total Revenue, Total Interest, and Income, Credit Score Segment.

- Revenue by Week segmented by Gender.

- Top 5 States for Revenue.

- Customer segmentation by Salary Group, Dependent Count, Marital Status, and Education Level.

- Age Group distribution by Gender.

- Card Category analysis with Sum of Revenue, Interest Earned, and Annual Fees.

## Graphs Used:
Variety of charts has been to visualize the data more effectively:

#### 1.Line Charts:
- Used to display trends over time, such as the "Revenue by Week" segmented by gender.

#### 2.Bar Charts:
- Utilized for categorical data comparison, such as "Revenue by Customer Job", "Revenue by Expenditure Type","Revenue by Education", "Revenue by Use Chip", and "Customer Acquisition Cost".
#### 3.Stacked Bar Charts:
- Show distribution within categories, such as the "Age Group", "Top 5 states","Salary Group","Dependent Count","Maritial status","Education level" distribution by gender.
#### 4.Table:
- Display detailed numerical data for "Card Category" analysis, including sum of revenue, interest earned, and annual fees.

#### 5.Slicer (Week Start Date):
- Filters data by selected weeks for detailed time-based analysis.

#### 6.Treemap (Quarters, Gender, Card Category, Income Group):
- Displays data categorized by quarters, gender, card category, and income group to highlight seasonal trends, segment contributions, and provide a holistic view of the data.

## ➡️ Functions Applied:

- CALCULATE: Modify the filter context of a calculation.
- SUM: Aggregate numerical values for total calculations.
- SUMX: Iterate over a table and sum the results.
- FILTER: Filter data based on specific conditions.
- RELATED: Retrieve related data from another table.
- IF: Perform conditional logic.
- DIVIDE: Perform division with error handling for division by zero.
- DATEADD: Add or subtract a specified number of days, months, quarters, or years from a date.
- DISTINCTCOUNT: Count the number of distinct values.
- AVERAGE: Calculate the average of numerical values.

## ➡️ Insights:
### Credit Card Customer Report 🔎
- Revenue Trends: Analysis shows revenue trends by week, highlighting seasonal variations and gender differences in spending.
- Demographics: Majority of high-revenue customers fall in the age groups of 30-40 and 40-50. Higher income levels are associated with higher revenues.
- Education Level: Graduates and post-graduates contribute significantly to the revenue.
  
### Credit Card Transaction Report 🔎
- Quarterly Performance: Q2 and Q3 show peak revenues and transaction volumes, indicating potential seasonal spikes.
- Customer Jobs: Businessmen and white-collar job holders are the highest contributors to revenue.
- Expenditure Types: Bills, entertainment, and fuel are the top expenditure types, indicating popular spending categories.
